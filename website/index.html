Purpose

What FURSOY Mail does

FURSOY Mail is a desktop email client supporting IMAP and major providers. With your permission, it reads and synchronizes your email messages so it can detect verification codes locally, show new-mail notifications, and let you copy codes without searching through your inbox.

Email account access enables the mail actions you choose, including sending messages, marking messages as read, and moving messages to trash. Mail data is processed on your device and is not sent to or stored on FURSOY servers.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does FURSOY Mail handle verification codes?

FURSOY Mail monitors your inbox locally over IMAP and secure APIs. When a verification code or OTP (one-time password) email arrives, it parses the code on your device and sends a native Windows notification with a single-click copy button.

Which email providers are supported?

FURSOY Mail supports all standard IMAP email providers (including Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, Fastmail, and custom domain IMAP servers).

Is my email data safe and private?

Yes. FURSOY Mail is local-first and open source. Your credentials and emails stay on your computer. Your email data is never sent to or stored on third-party FURSOY servers, and there is zero telemetry or tracking.

What Windows versions are supported?

FURSOY Mail is optimized for 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11 desktop operating systems.
